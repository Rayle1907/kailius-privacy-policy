# Kailius Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

### Who is responsible

Teodora Grumezescu is the data controller for Kailius (`com.kailius.gauntlet11`). For privacy questions, withdrawal, or deletion requests, contact **teog@itu.dk**.

### Information collected and its purpose

Kailius is used in a university study of player behaviour and gameplay decisions. With your permission, Unity Analytics collects a randomly generated player identifier, experiment assignment, and gameplay information, including progress, deaths, session timings, revive decisions, and Ember balances. Unity Analytics also processes technical information as described in its [privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

This information is used to study player behaviour and gameplay decisions. Records are pseudonymous, meaning they use identifiers rather than your name, but are not necessarily anonymous. Game progress, preferences, and consent choices are also stored on your device.

### Your choices

Participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw at any time by contacting **teog@itu.dk**. You can enable or disable analytics through the game’s privacy settings and request deletion using **Delete Analytics Data**. Disabling analytics does not delete previously collected information.

For deletion of research records held outside Unity Analytics, contact the email address above. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes local game data but does not delete previously submitted analytics.

### Processing and retention

Analytics data is processed through Unity Analytics and used for the study. Research data will be deleted by **28 February 2027**. Deletion requests submitted to Unity are processed according to Unity’s procedures.

### Your rights and policy changes

You may contact **teog@itu.dk** to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or to exercise other applicable data protection rights. You may also complain to the [Danish Data Protection Agency](https://www.datatilsynet.dk/english).

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
